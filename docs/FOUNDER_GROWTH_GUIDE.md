# 📰 Founder's Guide to GenZ Live Audience Growth & Newsroom Management

Welcome! As the founder of **GenZ Live ("The Voice of GenZ")**, this guide explains how to use your new growth tools every morning without touching any code.

---

## ☕ Every Morning Routine (5 Minutes)

1. **Log In to Admin CMS**: Visit **[https://genz-live.com/admin/newsroom](https://genz-live.com/admin/newsroom)**.
2. **Check "Today's Newsroom Daily Brief"**:
   - **Recommended Publishing Window**: Shows the best hours to post stories today based on audience activity.
   - **Today's Editorial Opportunities**: Lists stories gaining traffic that deserve follow-up reporting or refresh updates.
3. **Distribute Stories on Social Media**: Visit **[https://genz-live.com/admin/growth](https://genz-live.com/admin/growth)**.
   - Click any story package (X, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp).
   - Click **"Copy Text"** and paste directly into your social media account!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Google Discover?
Google Discover is the personalized news feed on mobile phones (Google App / Chrome homepage). Articles with high-resolution featured images and strong headlines get featured here.

### What is Search Console (GSC)?
Google Search Console tracks what search terms people type on Google before landing on GenZ Live.

### How do I enable Google AdSense?
When your AdSense account is approved, copy your Publisher ID (starts with `pub-`) and set `ADSENSE_PUBLISHER_ID="pub-xxx"` in your environment file. Your site will automatically serve `/ads.txt`!
